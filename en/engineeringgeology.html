Engineering Geology

Geotechnical works have to be built on natural materials such as rock and soil, and the properties of natural materials vary enormously. Anyone who wishes to sculpt the natural world must first understand its geology. Engineering geology, the bridge between geotechnical engineering and the geological sciences, is therefore a research topic of great importance.

Within engineering geology this laboratory concentrates on field investigation, the collection of engineering geological information, and the construction of databases. Field investigation covers the survey of discontinuity orientations in jointed rock masses, rock mass classification, and the preliminary estimation of the strength of rock material. As regards the collection of engineering geological information, we seek out engineering data from different regions of Taiwan in order to compile the mechanical properties of rock materials and of joint surfaces; the data are then analysed and assembled into an engineering geological database that can serve as a preliminary reference for design and construction organisations.
