Point Load Tester

The point load tester is a small instrument for rock mechanics testing, originally developed at Imperial College London. It consists of a two-column fixed crosshead frame together with a manual hydraulic jack. Pressure applied by the jack drives the conical platens forward and delivers a point load; from the load at which the specimen fails, the uniaxial compressive strength of the rock material can be estimated.

The tester of this laboratory is an EL-77 point load tester made by ELE, United Kingdom. Its maximum applied load is 55 kN and the largest specimen diameter it accepts is 101.6 mm.
